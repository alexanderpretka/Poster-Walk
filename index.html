Evaluation Instructions

Thank you for participating in the IDEA Ambassador Capstone Poster Walk. Please first listen to the IDEA Ambassador’s presentation, then complete this evaluation form based on their poster, presentation, and discussion. Each of the seven criteria is scored from 1 to 4, for a maximum of 28 points. You may use the suggested questions to guide your discussion with the IDEA Ambassador after their presentation has concluded.

This evaluation is intended to support learning, reflection, and professional development.

Part 1: Poster Content

Five criteria · 20 points possible

1. Understanding of System of Oppression

Explains the selected system of oppression, including relevant systems, structures, and historical or societal factors.

2. Knowledge and Application of Learning

Applies ideas from the Context, Voices, and Next Steps chapters and demonstrates critical thinking about the topic.

3. Connection to Student Experiences

Connects the topic to student experiences, higher education, or the campus environment using relevant examples.

4. Reflective Questions and Professional Application

Encourages reflection on staff practices, policies, and professional responsibilities related to the topic.

5. Poster Organization, Design, and References

Uses a clear, readable, professional layout and includes appropriate references formatted in APA 7 style.

Part 2: Presentation and Discussion

Two criteria · 8 points possible

6. Oral Presentation and Communication

Delivers a prepared 2–3 minute presentation and communicates ideas clearly and accessibly.

7. Discussion and Engagement

Facilitates discussion, responds thoughtfully to questions, and remains open to different perspectives.

Suggested Questions for Evaluators

Use these questions to encourage discussion and reflection with the IDEA Ambassador after their presentation.

  1. Why did you choose this system of oppression?
  2. How does this system of oppression affect individuals / communities?
  3. What was the most important thing you learned while completing this project?
  4. How does your topic connect to the experiences of on-campus PSU students?
  5. What assumptions / perspectives did you reconsider during this project?
  6. How might you apply what you learned in your future professional role?
  7. What sources most influenced your understanding of this topic?
  8. What is one thing you want evaluators to take away from your poster?
  9. How might you use what you learned to create a more inclusive environment?
  10. What additional questions do you still have about your topic?
